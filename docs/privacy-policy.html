PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: May 20, 2026
GoalPulse ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our Android application GoalPulse (the "App"). By using GoalPulse, you agree to the terms described here.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you interact with the App (screens viewed, features used), collected via Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash Reports: Diagnostic crash and error data collected via Firebase Crashlytics to help us improve app stability.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and language preference to personalise your experience.
  • Push Notification Tokens: A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token, generated automatically when you install the App, used to send match alerts and goal notifications.
  • Preferences: Your in-app settings (dark mode preference, selected language) stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information (PII) unless you explicitly provide it.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively to:

  • Deliver live match scores, goal alerts, and tournament updates via push notifications.
  • Improve app performance and fix crashes using anonymous diagnostics.
  • Understand feature usage to prioritise improvements.
  • Restore your language and appearance preferences between sessions.

4. Third-Party Services

GoalPulse uses the following third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services – policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Retention

Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to their own retention policies (typically 2 months). Local preferences stored on your device are cleared when you uninstall the App.

6. Children's Privacy

GoalPulse is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security practices, including HTTPS-only network communication (enforced via Android Network Security Config), to protect the data transmitted by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; however, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device's notification settings.
  • Disable analytics collection by opting out inside the App's Profile → Preferences section.
  • Request deletion of any personal data we hold about you by contacting us below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of GoalPulse after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
